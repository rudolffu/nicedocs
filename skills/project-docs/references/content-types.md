# Content types: FAQs and release notes

Keep each content type consistent, so readers know what a page of that type will
give them.

## FAQs
An FAQ is short and limited. It covers need-to-know information and points to fuller
pages. An effective FAQ:
- reflects real audience needs, for example the search queries that lead to the docs
- is updated as user behavior and data change
- sends readers on to detailed pages
- holds topics too small to justify their own page.

**Maintenance warning:** content gets parked in FAQs and goes stale. Give the FAQ a
review schedule, and move answers into proper pages when they grow.

## Release notes
Release notes give users what they need to keep using and benefiting from the product
after a release. Keep them brief, and link out for details.

Checklist for each entry:
1. What exactly changed?
2. Why did it change, and why does it matter to users? (Workflow or UI improvement,
   consistency or feature parity, policy or legal change, security.)
3. What does this let users do, or what should they do now?
4. Do users have everything they need to proceed, such as migration steps or breaking
   changes?
5. Is there a fuller article? Link to it.
6. Would an image or short example help?
7. Who must approve the entry (for example legal, for policy changes)?

Template: [../assets/release-notes.template.md](../assets/release-notes.template.md).

Avoid release notes that say nothing, such as "bug fixes and performance improvements"
with no detail.
