# Reducing bias and writing inclusively

Reduce bias by thinking about what your words mean, where they come from, and how
readers might take them. Even the example user names you choose make a difference.

## Review checklist
- [ ] **Loaded terminology.** Prefer neutral, descriptive terms, for example
      allowlist/denylist, primary/replica, main branch. If a technical term must stay,
      for example because it is in an API name, use it only where needed and explain it.
- [ ] **Gendered defaults.** Use singular "they" or rewrite in second person. Avoid
      "guys" and gendered job titles.
- [ ] **Ableist and violent language.** Avoid "sanity check", "dummy value", "cripple",
      and "kill two birds…". Prefer "quick check", "placeholder value", "disable", and
      "accomplish two things".
- [ ] **Assumed knowledge or ease.** Drop "simply", "just", "easy", and "obviously". They
      tell struggling readers they are failing.
- [ ] **Culture-specific references.** Idioms, sports metaphors, holidays, and units
      should be literal or explained.
- [ ] **Example names and data.** Use names, places, and personas from many regions and
      cultures. Avoid stereotyped roles, such as always a male admin and a female assistant.
